Supports the four named motors, literal powers from −1 to 1, sleep(1–60000), and comments. Each sleep creates a timed step. Motor powers carry forward between steps. Export adds hardware mapping, direction setup, and a stop routine.

75 mm is REV’s standard DUO mecanum wheel. The HD Hex motor is assumed. The 3:1 × 4:1 × 5:1 UltraPlanetary cartridges give an actual 54.8329:1 reduction. External reduction is motor-output rotations per wheel turn.
